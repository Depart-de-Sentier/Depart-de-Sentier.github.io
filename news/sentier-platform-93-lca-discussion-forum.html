Scaling reliable LCA through automation: the Sentier Platform at the 93rd LCA Discussion Forum

Behind it lies a messy trail of raw data inputs. Right now, the infrastructure to capture exactly how those inputs became a dataset doesn't exist. Documentation is almost always insufficient. If something changes, tracking the provenance of that change is time-consuming, difficult, or sometimes impossible.

We also lack a community-centered vocabulary to link terms across different databases, making cross-comparison a constant headache. Worst of all, when users find errors, their feedback takes a long time to incorporate, or simply gets deprioritized or ignored.

Laurenz Bougan from Départ de Sentier addressed these structural bottlenecks last week at the 93rd LCA Discussion Forum (#93LCADF) in Zurich: Scaling Reliable LCA through Automation: Expectations, Best Practices and Lessons Learned, by presenting our Sentier Platform: a backbone of a scalable and flexible LCA data pipeline.

Instead of automating a broken, opaque process, we are building open infrastructure for LCI dataset construction, publication, sharing and maintenance. During the session, Laurenz shared a showcase, thanks to the funding by ADEME, demonstrating how modules in the Sentier Platform create a flexible pipeline to make Agribalyse available in Brightway quickly and transparently.

On top of separating raw data inputs from the models used to generate LCI datasets, we can add provenance, cross links among terminologies of different systems, and build an infrastructure that is designed to promptly take into account community feedback.

Automation shouldn't just mean doing things faster. It means starting with clean data structure and making the process robust and open from the start.
