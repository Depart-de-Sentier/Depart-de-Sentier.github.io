Brightcon 2026: Inspirations, Quality Content and Open Discussions

Brightcon 2026 has come to a close, and what a week it was. From September 20 to 25, the open-source LCA community gathered at Aalborg University, Denmark, and online around the world, to share ideas, code, questions and discussions about transparent, accessible LCA-based sustainability assessment.

Many conversations revealed significant potential for synergies. They also showed that, with the fast pace of AI-assisted coding, teams are increasingly building customized, sector-specific versions of very similar applications in parallel. This led to a broader discussion: what would a more collaborative environment look like, and what steps does the community need to take to make it possible?

Inspiration. Hackathon sprints, hands-on courses and keynotes reminded us how much creativity sits in this community. Newcomers and long-time contributors worked side by side, and many left with new ideas, new collaborations and a longer to-do list than they arrived with.

Quality content. Across three days of conference sessions, speakers presented the latest developments in LCA-based open sustainability assessment: new tools and data, methods, and lessons learned from real projects. The high standard of the talks is a credit to everyone who submitted, reviewed and presented.

Open discussions. Some of the best moments happened during workshops, panel discussions and coffee breaks between and after sessions. Hybrid participation meant that colleagues from different time zones could take part in the same conversations, and the questions and critical feedback made the content better.

Our heartfelt thanks go to our cohost team, led by Prof. Massimo Pizzol from the Department of Sustainability and Planning at Aalborg University, to all speakers, session chairs and volunteers, and to everyone who joined, in person or online.

The conference materials are now available for everyone: Brightcon 2026 conference materials. You can find all conference programme details on the Brightcon 2026 website.

Recordings are available to both online and onsite participants right after the conference. Recordings of speakers who have given their consent will be made available to the public for free after a one-month embargo period. Stay tuned!

See you at the next Brightcon!
