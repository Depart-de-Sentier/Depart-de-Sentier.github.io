Xiaojin Zhang

Xiaojin Zhang is a sustainability and decarbonization specialist with more than 15 years of experience across consulting and academia. Her expertise spans Life Cycle Assessment (LCA), techno-economic analysis, system dynamics modeling and geospatial analysis, applied to buildings, energy, food and agriculture. She has led multidisciplinary projects, secured funding, and advised industry and government on data-driven decarbonization strategies. Today she leads the daily operation of Départ de Sentier, building open infrastructure for life cycle inventory data and fostering community exchange within the Brightway ecosystem.
